FAQ

Common questions

Is CreditFlow a lender or a wallet?
This web portal records and displays merchant and lender account information. It does not transfer funds or split payment settlements.
Does the shop need a new QR code?
The current web portal does not manage or issue QR codes. Sign in to view the contract and transaction features connected to the backend.
Does the portal process payments?
No. Merchant transaction records and lender repayment updates are saved to the backend, but the portal does not initiate or settle payments.
Is this connected to real money or Truecaller?
The portals read account, contract and transaction records from the connected backend. Recording a transaction stores it in the backend; it does not initiate a payment or settlement.
How do I access a portal?
Sign in with your backend username and password, then choose Merchant or Lender to open the matching account.
